* **pharmacological_findings:**
  * Antioxidant activity: High DPPH and ABTS radical scavenging activity in ethanol/methanol extracts ([42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p38_c3], [42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p38_c2]).
  * Anti-inflammatory: Curcumin and essential oil (turmerones) exhibit significant anti-inflammatory effects in vitro and in vivo ([42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p39_c1], [42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p35_c1]).
  * Antimicrobial: Ethanolic extracts show antibacterial activity against Staphylococcus aureus and other pathogens ([42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p38_c1]).
  * Anti-melanogenesis: Curcuminoid analogs inhibit tyrosinase and melanin production in B16F10 mouse melanoma cells ([42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p35_c1], [42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p36_c1]).
  * Cytotoxicity: Curcumin and extracts show cytotoxic effects on cancer cell lines ([42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p39_c1]).
  * Safety: Subchronic toxicity studies (90 days, rats) show no significant adverse effects at tested doses ([42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p40_c1], [42_KhaMinChun.pdf_p39_c2]).
