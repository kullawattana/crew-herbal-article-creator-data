    * **location:** Prachinburi Province
    * **ethnic_group:** -
* **community_profile_summaries:**
    * **community_highlight:** Abhaibhubejhr Thai Traditional Medicine Museum serves as a central hub for the collection, preservation, and dissemination of knowledge about Thai traditional medicine and herbs. Local communities have benefited economically from shifting to herbal cultivation, with increased family incomes, and the museum's products are recognized for their quality and research-backed efficacy.
    * **name_etymology:** No herbal-related information found.

    * **local_wisdom:** The local wisdom preserved by the museum encompasses traditional Thai medical recipes and the integration of herbal medicines in daily healthcare. By making herbal treatments accessible and combining them with conventional medicine, the community continues to value and rely on indigenous herbal knowledge for health and wellness.
    * **community_organizations:** The museum operates under the Chao Phraya Abhaibhubejhr Foundation, which oversees the production and distribution of traditional medicines. The foundation ensures quality control and research, supporting sustainable herbal practices and providing economic opportunities for local farmers and producers.

    * **name_etymology:** No herbal-related information found.
    * **community_environment:** The community is rooted in traditional agriculture, with residents cultivating fruit orchards and local herbs. Herbs like citronella, turmeric, neem leaves, and cinnamon are grown and used as natural raw materials, reflecting the community's close relationship with their environment and commitment to sustainable practices.
    * **local_wisdom:** Local wisdom is demonstrated through the adaptation and continuation of traditional herbal incense-making, herbal water, and spray production. These practices are passed down through generations, ensuring the survival of indigenous knowledge and providing new avenues for economic development.
    * **community_organizations:** The establishment of the Ban Thoop Hom Samunphrai community enterprise has formalized local herbal production. The group organizes workshops, product development, and skill training for both residents and visitors, fostering community engagement and knowledge sharing.
