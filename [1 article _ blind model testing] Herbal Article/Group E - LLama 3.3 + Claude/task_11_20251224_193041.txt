* **Toxicity Fact (Summary):** Turmeric and its extract curcumin are generally safe but have recently been linked, especially in high-bioavailability forms, to rare cases of immune-mediated acute liver injury that typically resolve after stopping use, though severe outcomes can occur if use continues.
* **Toxicity Fact (Mechanism):** The exact mechanism of liver injury is not well understood, but it is thought to be related to the immune system's response to curcumin.
* **Toxicity Fact (Side Effects):** There is no scientific evidence that curcumin reduces inflammation, and turmeric supplements are associated with rare but potentially serious liver injuries, particularly in genetically susceptible individuals.
* **Toxicity Fact (Case Reports):** There have been reports of liver injury in individuals taking turmeric supplements, but the exact number of cases is not well established.
