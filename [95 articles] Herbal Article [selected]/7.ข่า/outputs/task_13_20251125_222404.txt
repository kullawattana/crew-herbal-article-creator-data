# Galangal: From Ancient Wisdom to Modern Strategy
## Herbal in Wellness Trends
Galangal is a spice native to Southern Asia, closely related to ginger and turmeric, and has been used in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine for centuries to treat various ailments, including infections, inflammation, and cancer. The root of galangal is rich in antioxidants and may boost male fertility, reduce inflammation and pain, and protect against infections and certain types of cancer. As a popular remedy in traditional medicine, galangal can add flavor, antioxidants, and anti-inflammatory compounds to dishes, providing a slew of health benefits. Its use as a natural and traditional remedy has been growing in interest, making it an attractive ingredient for new product development.

## Scientific Deep-Dive
Alpinia galanga, the scientific name for galangal, has been studied for its bioactive compounds and potential health benefits. The study on Alpinia galanga rhizome extract (GRE) highlights its cardioprotective effects and antioxidant properties, suggesting that galangal extracts could be used as a viable ingredient in new product development. 
> **Scientific Research:** 
> This study evaluated the cardioprotective effects of Alpinia galanga rhizome extract (GRE) against noise-induced myocardial injury via phytochemical profiling, molecular docking, and in vivo assessment. Male Wistar rats (n = 6/group) were assigned to the following four groups: control (C), control + GRE (100 mg/kg), noise-exposed (N), and noise-exposed + GRE (N+GRE, 100 mg/kg). Rats in the N and N+GRE groups were exposed to 90 dB(A) white noise for 2 h/day for 28 days, with GRE administered orally throughout the exposure period. Phytochemical analysis confirmed the presence of flavonoids and phenolic acids with known antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activities. In vitro, GRE significantly reduced nitric oxide production in lipopolysaccharide-stimulated RAW264.7 macrophages. In vivo, noise exposure elevated cardiac malondialdehyde levels, impaired antioxidant enzyme activity, and increased circulating tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-alpha) and heme oxygenase-1 (HO-1) levels. GRE treatment restored redox balance, suppressed proinflammatory mediator levels, and improved histopathological alterations. Molecular docking analysis indicated strong binding of GRE phytoconstituents to HO-1 and TNF-alpha, supporting the observed in vivo effects. These findings demonstrate that GRE mitigates noise-induced cardiac injury through its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, highlighting its therapeutic potential.

## Traditional Wisdom
The Bru, Akha, and Kha communities have a strong tradition of using galangal in their daily lives. The Bru community is known for their expertise in handicrafts and basketry, and they use galangal in their traditional medicine. The Akha community has a strong tradition of agriculture and has migrated from Tibet to various parts of Southeast Asia, using galangal as a type of herb that is commonly used in Thai cuisine and traditional medicine. The Kha community is also known for their expertise in handicrafts and basketry, and they use galangal in their traditional medicine. Galangal is used in various traditional recipes, such as "คั่วบานะบายข้าวกิน" which is a dish made with galangal, pork, and other ingredients.
