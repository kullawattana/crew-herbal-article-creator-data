They have a unique way of life, including their food gathering methods, shelter construction, beliefs, and rituals. However, some Sakai groups have started to settle down and intermarry with locals, leading to an increase in mixed Sakai descendants. Despite adapting to urban culture, some Sakai groups still face challenges in adjusting to certain aspects of city life.

However, their lack of formal education and the disregard for their rights by outsiders have made them vulnerable to exploitation.
