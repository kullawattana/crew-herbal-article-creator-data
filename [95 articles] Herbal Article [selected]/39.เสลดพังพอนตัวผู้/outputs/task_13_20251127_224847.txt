## Scientific Deep-Dive
Barleria lupulina, also known as Hop Headed Barleria, has been studied for its potential health benefits. The herb contains compounds such as barlerin and verbascoside, which have been shown to boost immunity and regulate CYP450 genes in prostate cancer. 
> **Scientific Research:** 
> Barleria extracts containing barlerin and verbascoside boost immunity and regulate CYP450 gene in prostate cancer. The findings indicate that two key metabolites, barlerin and verbascoside, are present in all six Barleria extracts, with B. siamensis exhibiting the highest amount of these compounds at 0.43 mg/g (barlerin) and 1.02 mg/g (verbascoside) of dried leaf, respectively. In terms of toxicological effects, B. cristata and B. siamensis demonstrated significant anti-proliferative activity against PC-3 cells by inducing DNA damage, enhancing apoptosis, and obstructing the cell cycle. However, these extracts did not exert cytotoxic effects on PBMCs, HPrEC, and THLE-3. Conversely, B. strigosa extract exhibited mild toxicity towards HPrECs and moderate toxicity towards THLE-3 cells. Furthermore, treatment with these extracts activated PBMCs, leading to the upregulation of cytokine genes, including IL-2, IL-10, IL-12, IL-15, IL-21, and IFN-γ, which promoted cytotoxicity for PC-3 cells. Additionally, B. siamensis extract significantly suppressed the expression of CYP450 genes, including CYP1A2, CYP3A4, CYP2D6, and CYP2E1, whereas B. strigosa extract induced the overexpression of CYP2E1.

## Traditional Wisdom
The Dong Bang Community in Prachinburi Province, Thailand, has a rich tradition of using herbal medicine, including Barleria lupulina. The community is known for its fertile soil, making it suitable for growing herbs and other crops. The local name for Barleria lupulina is "Male Phang Pho" (เสลดพังพอนตัวผู้), and it is used in traditional Thai medicine. The community has a strong tradition of using natural remedies to treat various health conditions, and they have established a hospital that combines traditional herbal medicine with modern medical practices.
