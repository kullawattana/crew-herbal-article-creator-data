The use of sesame in Thai cuisine is believed to have originated from the ancient practice of using sesame oil as a form of medicine. Sesame oil is still used today in traditional Thai medicine to treat a range of ailments, including skin conditions and respiratory problems. Furthermore, sesame is also used in traditional Thai rituals and ceremonies, where it is believed to have spiritual significance. In these contexts, sesame is often used as an offering to the gods or as a symbol of good fortune and prosperity.
