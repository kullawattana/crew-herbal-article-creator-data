# Clinacanthus nutans: From Traditional Wisdom to Modern Wellness
## Herbal in Wellness Trends
Clinacanthus nutans, commonly known as Snake Jasmine, has been a cornerstone of traditional medicine in Southeast Asia, particularly for its anti-inflammatory, antipyretic, and detoxicant properties. It is used to treat various diseases, including skin allergies, diabetes, hypertension, and cancer. The leaves of Snake Jasmine are widely used in Ayurveda for easing arthritis, joint stiffness, and back pain. Moreover, the plant is valued for its ability to treat snake venom and its numerous uses as an antioxidant powerhouse. With its rich history in traditional medicine and its potential health benefits, Clinacanthus nutans presents a significant market opportunity for the development of new herbal products.

## Traditional Wisdom
The use of Clinacanthus nutans is deeply rooted in traditional Thai medicine, where it is known as "เสลดพังพอนตัวเมีย". The community of Ban Phatthasima in Nakhon Si Thammarat Province is particularly known for its rich cultural heritage in traditional medicine, with a long history of using herbal remedies documented in ancient texts. The leaves of the Luktan plant, another name for Clinacanthus nutans in local wisdom, are used to treat fever by boiling them in water and drinking the resulting tea. This traditional knowledge highlights the importance of Clinacanthus nutans in local health practices and its potential for modern wellness applications.
