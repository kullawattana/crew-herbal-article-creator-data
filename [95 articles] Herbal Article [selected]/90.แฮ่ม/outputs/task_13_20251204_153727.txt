## Traditional Wisdom
The Yong and Lue communities have traditionally used Coscinium fenestratum, known locally as Khamin Chan, for relieving stomach pain. This traditional use highlights the cultural significance of the herb in Thai traditional medicine. The communities' reliance on agriculture and forestry, and their deep understanding of traditional medicine, demonstrate a strong connection with nature and a holistic approach to health. The use of Coscinium fenestratum in these communities also reflects a broader cultural heritage, where traditional practices and customs are highly valued.
