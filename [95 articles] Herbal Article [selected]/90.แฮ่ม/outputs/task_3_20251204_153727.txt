### Overall Summary
* **summary:** Coscinium fenestratum is a medicinal plant that has been traditionally used in Southeast Asia for its anti-inflammatory properties. The plant contains berberine, a compound that has been shown to have anti-inflammatory effects. Studies have used macro-microscopy, DNA barcoding, and HPTLC to assess the quality of berberine-containing botanicals traded as Maramanjal/Daruharidra. The results of these studies have revealed the presence of berberine in all 14 samples, with varying concentrations.
