            * **description_quote:** "Making miang is the main occupation of the villagers, known as 'miang cultivation'. The villagers plant miang seeds in the rainy season and let them grow on their own. However, the yield is low, so they prefer to plant them in rows along the slopes of the mountains. They take care of the plants for about 3 years before harvesting the leaves. The leaves are harvested for about 5-6 months, from August to February. The villagers select the middle-sized leaves, which are not too young or too old. After harvesting the fresh leaves, they are placed in a wooden container and steamed for about 1 hour. Then, they are left to cool before being fermented in a fermentation container for about 1 month. After that, the leaves are ready to be used with salt."
