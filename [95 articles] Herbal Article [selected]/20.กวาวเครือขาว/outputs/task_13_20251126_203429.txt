## Traditional Wisdom
Pueraria mirifica has been used in traditional Thai medicine for a long time, particularly in the Ban Natong community located in Phrae Province. The villagers have a strong connection with nature and have developed unique traditional practices to live in harmony with the environment. The herb is used to enhance bust size and is believed to have various health benefits, including balancing the body's elements and aiding in digestion. The traditional use of Pueraria mirifica in Thai medicine highlights its potential as a natural wellness product.
