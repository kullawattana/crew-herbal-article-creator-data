# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Citrus hystrix * **herb_name:** Citrus hystrix * **source_browsed:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548447/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** The liver injury attributed to Herbalife products remains unexplained. There was no clear commonality in the products themselves, much less their constituents. Some products contained green tea or aloe vera extracts, both which have been implicated in other forms of HDS liver injury. * **mechanism_of_injury:** The mechanism of liver injury that has been attributed to Herbalife products is not well understood. The injury is typically hepatocellular and can be severe, but the specific substances responsible are unknown. * **reported_side_effects:** The reported side effects of Herbalife products include nausea, epigastric pain, jaundice, and elevated liver enzymes. * **case_report_summary:** There have been several case reports of liver injury attributed to Herbalife products, including a case of a 37-year-old woman who developed jaundice and elevated liver enzymes after taking multiple Herbalife products for weight loss.
