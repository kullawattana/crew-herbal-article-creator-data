## Traditional Wisdom
Aegle marmelos has been used for healing in Thai wisdom for a long time. It is believed to have various health benefits, including aiding in digestion and relieving symptoms of certain illnesses. In the community of Ban Dong Bang, Aegle marmelos is used to relieve stomach pain, while in Ban Khao Yai, it is used to relieve fever. The traditional practice of using Aegle marmelos is passed down through generations, with local healers developing a unique understanding of the medicinal properties of plants.
