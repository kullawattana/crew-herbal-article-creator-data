## Strategic Plan for New Product Development Based on Master Fact Sheet
* **Market Opportunity:** The Master Fact Sheet provides insights into the traditional use of Allium sativum (Garlic) in Thai medicine and cuisine, highlighting its potential as a natural remedy for various health issues. The fact that garlic is a staple ingredient in many Mon dishes and is used to treat ailments such as fever and rheumatism suggests a market opportunity for developing garlic-based products that cater to the growing demand for natural and traditional remedies. Additionally, the presence of garlic in traditional Thai medicine and its use in combination with other herbs to create remedies for various health issues indicates a potential market for garlic-based products that target specific health concerns.

However, further research and development would be necessary to determine the technical feasibility of developing new garlic-based products.

It also mentions that the product should not be used if the pellet dispenser seal is broken and contains approximately 80 pellets. Additionally, the fact sheet provides warnings, such as stopping use and asking a doctor if symptoms persist for more than 3 days or worsen. However, there are no explicit toxicity facts or safety warnings related to the use of garlic, suggesting that garlic is generally considered safe for consumption. Nevertheless, it is essential to conduct thorough safety and toxicity assessments to ensure the safe use of garlic-based products.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Based on the Master Fact Sheet, a strategic recommendation for new product development would be to focus on creating garlic-based products that cater to the growing demand for natural and traditional remedies. This could include developing products that target specific health concerns, such as digestive issues or respiratory problems, and incorporating other natural ingredients to enhance the efficacy and safety of the products. Additionally, it would be essential to conduct thorough safety and toxicity assessments to ensure the safe use of garlic-based products and to comply with regulatory requirements. Partnering with traditional medicine experts and herbalists could also provide valuable insights and expertise in developing effective and safe garlic-based products.
