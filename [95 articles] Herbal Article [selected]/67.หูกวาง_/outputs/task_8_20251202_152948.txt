	+ **location:** Prachuap Khiri Khan Province
	+ **ethnic_group:** Fishing Community
* **community_profile_summaries:**
	+ **community_highlight:** Bang Pu Village is a fishing community located on the coast of the Gulf of Thailand. The community has several natural and cultural tourist attractions, including Bang Pu Beach, Laem Sala Beach, Kaeo Cave, and Phraya Nakhon Cave, which is home to the Kuha Karuhas Palace, a pavilion built by King Chulalongkorn (Rama V) during his visit to the Malay Peninsula.

	+ **community_environment:** The community is surrounded by natural attractions, including beaches and caves, which are popular tourist destinations.
