# ===CULTURAL_INTERNAL_SUMMARY=== Cardamom, also known as กระวาน in Thai, has been used for centuries in traditional Thai medicine and cooking. It is believed to have various health benefits, including aiding digestion and relieving symptoms of indigestion and nausea. In Thai cuisine, cardamom is often used as a spice and is a key ingredient in many dishes, including curries and desserts. It is also used in traditional Thai medicine to treat a range of ailments, including respiratory problems and skin conditions. Additionally, cardamom is believed to have antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, which can help to protect against cell damage and reduce inflammation. Overall, cardamom is a versatile and valuable herb that has been used for centuries in Thai culture for its culinary, medicinal, and cultural significance.
