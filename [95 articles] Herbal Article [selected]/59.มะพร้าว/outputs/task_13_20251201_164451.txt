## Traditional Wisdom
Coconut (มะพร้าว) has been used for healing in Thai wisdom for a long time. It is believed to have various health benefits, including aiding in digestion and relieving symptoms of certain illnesses. In traditional Thai medicine, coconut is used to treat various ailments, such as fever, cough, and skin conditions. The oil extracted from coconuts is also used in cooking and as a natural remedy for hair and skin care. Additionally, coconut water is consumed as a refreshing beverage and is believed to have detoxifying properties. The community of Ban Suan Thung, located in Samut Songkhram Province, has been making coconut sugar for a long time, and it is their main occupation. The process of boiling coconut sugar uses consistent fire for about 1 hour and 30 minutes, the sugar will slowly thicken until it becomes yellow or what is called "dok mai", it will become small bubbles, boiling further for a while, the bubbles will become bigger, indicating that it is ready.
