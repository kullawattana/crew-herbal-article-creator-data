# ===CULTURAL_INTERNAL_SUMMARY=== Coconut (มะพร้าว) has been used for healing in Thai wisdom for a long time. It is believed to have various health benefits, including aiding in digestion and relieving symptoms of certain illnesses. In traditional Thai medicine, coconut is used to treat various ailments, such as fever, cough, and skin conditions. The oil extracted from coconuts is also used in cooking and as a natural remedy for hair and skin care. Additionally, coconut water is consumed as a refreshing beverage and is believed to have detoxifying properties. Overall, coconut is considered a versatile and nutritious food in Thai culture, and its various uses are deeply rooted in traditional Thai wisdom.
