# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Momordica cochinchinensis * **herb_name:** Momordica cochinchinensis * **source_browsed:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7687717/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** The water extract of Momordica cochinchinensis aril caused significant apoptosis and necrosis of breast cancer and melanoma cells, with varieties from Northern Vietnam possessing superior activity. This highlights the potential of this fruit in the development of novel anticancer agents against such tumours, with specific regions on where to collect the best variety and extraction solvent for optimum activity. * **mechanism_of_injury:** The mechanism of cell death induced by the aril extract was found to be both apoptotic and necrotic, with typical biochemical and morphological characteristics. * **reported_side_effects:** No side effects were reported in the study. * **case_report_summary:** No case reports were mentioned in the study.
