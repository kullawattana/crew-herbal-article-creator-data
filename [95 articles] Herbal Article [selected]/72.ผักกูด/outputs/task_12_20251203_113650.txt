The strategic plan must include comprehensive research into the safety profile of Diplazium esculentum, including its compounds like kaempferol, to address any potential constraints or risks associated with its use.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Based on the analysis of the Master Fact Sheet, the strategic recommendation is to pursue the development of Diplazium esculentum-based products, particularly those targeting cognitive health or Alzheimer's disease, given the synergistic effects observed with donepezil.
