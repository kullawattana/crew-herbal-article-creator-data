            * **abstract_raw:** Optimal drying method is important for the quality preservation of green pepper (GP). This study assessed the effects of five drying methods on the color, structure, flavor, and nutritional properties of dried GP. Results indicated that freeze drying (FD) preserved excellent color (L = 56.27, a = -3.76) and microstructure through low-temperature sublimation, while thermal drying (hot air drying (HAD) and far-infrared drying (FIRD)) enhanced the aroma release via melanoidin and lipid oxidation reactions. Notably, FD-FIRD significantly reduced the energy consumption and drying time by 50.98% and 55.6%, respectively, compared to FD alone. This combined drying method also maintained a high level of piperine (6.75 mg/g) and flavonoids retention ratio (370.53%), as well as preserving good antioxidant activity. Notably, the 25 dominant VOCs exhibited inverse correlations with piperine and phenols (r = -0.54 to -0.92). In short, FD-FIRD is a promising dehydration process.
            * **key_findings:** The study found that freeze drying and far-infrared drying methods preserved the quality of green pepper, including its color, structure, flavor, and nutritional properties. The combined drying method also maintained a high level of piperine and flavonoids retention ratio, as well as preserving good antioxidant activity.

            * **abstract_raw:** Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a chronic inflammatory disease whose exact etiology is yet unidentified. Dexamethasone sodium phosphate (DSP) is an established anti-inflammatory agent, but its application is limited due to its dose-dependent toxicity.
