* **source_browsed:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK608429/

### Clinical Summary

* **toxicity_summary:** Tinospora crispa has been associated with cases of acute liver injury, with a hepatocellular pattern and sometimes a severe course. The injury is thought to be immunologically mediated, and the chemical compound responsible is believed to be a diterpenoid.
* **mechanism_of_injury:** The mechanism of injury is not fully understood, but it is thought to be related to the herb's ability to activate immune function and restore immune health.
* **reported_side_effects:** Side effects are uncommon but may include headache, dizziness, nausea, abdominal discomfort, diarrhea, and rash.
* **case_report_summary:** There have been several case reports of liver injury associated with Tinospora crispa, including cases of acute hepatitis and liver failure. The cases have been reported in various countries, including India, Thailand, and the United States.
