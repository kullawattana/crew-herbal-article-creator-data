## Traditional Wisdom
In traditional Thai medicine, Tinospora crispa is known by different local names, such as "Khamin Chan" in Ban Cham Pla Lai and "Luek Dee Plee" in Ban Dong Bang. The communities of Ban Cham Pla Lai and Ban Dong Bang, located in Chanthaburi Province, have a deep understanding of the local flora and fauna, which has been passed down through generations. Tinospora crispa is used to relieve stomach pain in Ban Cham Pla Lai, where it is processed through drying, and to treat fever in Ban Dong Bang, where it is processed through steaming. The rich cultural heritage of these communities highlights the importance of preserving traditional knowledge and practices related to herbal medicine.
