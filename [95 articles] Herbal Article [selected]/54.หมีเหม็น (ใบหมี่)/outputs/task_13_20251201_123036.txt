## Scientific Deep-Dive into Litsea glutinosa
Litsea glutinosa, a species of interest due to its bioactive compounds like tetradectetraenoic acid ethyl ester, has been investigated for its chemical composition and biological activities. The essential oils from Litsea species have shown good radical scavenging activity, metal chelating, and reducing power, along with significant anti-inflammatory and antibacterial properties. 

> **Scientific Research:** 
> The essential oils from four Litsea species of Uttarakhand were investigated for their chemical composition, phytochemical insights, and biological activities. The major compounds identified in the studied EOs of Litsea species were tetradectetraenoic acid ethyl ester, furanodiene, and E-β-caryophyllene. The EOs exhibited good radical scavenging activity against DPPH with IC50 values. All the tested EOs showed activity in the metal chelating and reducing power assay. Nitric oxide and superoxide radical scavenging were also analyzed and found to be significant. Anti-inflammatory activity was investigated in vitro and found best in the first chemotype of L. umbrosa with an IC50 value of 12.99 ± 0.140 μg/mL compared to standard diclofenac sodium (1.93 ± 0.033 μg/mL). Antibacterial activity was investigated against four pathogenic bacteria, namely, Salmonella typhimurium, Escherichia coli, Staphylococcus aureus, and Bacillus megaterium. All the EOs showed inhibition against the tested bacterial strains. Herbicidal activity was investigated for the first time for this species against Raphnus sativus seeds, where 100% inhibition was shown by L. monopetala EO.

The Lao Klang community, located in the Nakhon Pathom Province, has a rich cultural heritage and traditional practices.
