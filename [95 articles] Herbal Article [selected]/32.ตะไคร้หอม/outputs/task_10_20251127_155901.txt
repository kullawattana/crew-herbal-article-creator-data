# ===CULTURAL_INTERNAL_SUMMARY=== Lemongrass, also known as ตะไคร้หอม in Thai, has been used for centuries in Thai traditional medicine and cooking. It is known for its numerous health benefits, including its ability to aid digestion, relieve stress, and boost the immune system. In Thai cuisine, lemongrass is often used in soups, curries, and other dishes for its unique flavor and aroma. It is also used in traditional Thai medicine to treat various ailments, such as fever, cough, and sore throat. Additionally, lemongrass is believed to have antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, which can help to protect against chronic diseases. Overall, lemongrass is a versatile and valuable herb in Thai culture, and its uses extend beyond the kitchen to traditional medicine and health remedies.
