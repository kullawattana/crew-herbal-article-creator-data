# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Myristica fragrans * **herb_name:** Myristica fragrans * **source_browsed:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501838/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** High doses (e.g., a spoonful) of nutmeg can cause intoxication that includes anticholinergic symptoms. Anticholinergics may reduce lactation. Nutmeg and mace in amounts higher than those found in foods as a flavoring should be avoided during breastfeeding. * **mechanism_of_injury:** Anticholinergic symptoms, which may reduce lactation. * **reported_side_effects:** Anticholinergic symptoms. * **case_report_summary:** No data exist on the safety and efficacy of nutmeg in nursing mothers or infants.
