## Traditional Wisdom
The traditional use of Myristica fragrans in Thai herbal medicine is well-documented. Communities like Ban Dong Bang and Ban Khok Sa-at have a rich tradition of using local herbs, including Myristica fragrans, to treat various health problems. In Ban Dong Bang, Myristica fragrans is known as "Khamin Chan" and is used to relieve stomach pain, while in Ban Khok Sa-at, it is known as "Bai Mon" and is used to treat fever and headache. The processing methods vary, with drying being used in Ban Dong Bang and crushing in Ban Khok Sa-at. These traditional practices highlight the cultural significance of Myristica fragrans and its potential as a basis for modern health solutions.

## Safety, Regulatory, and Constraints
