Given the traditional use of Myristica fragrans in Thai herbal medicine, products could also be designed with cultural sensitivity, potentially incorporating traditional knowledge into modern health solutions. Overall, a cautious and research-driven approach is necessary to capitalize on the market opportunity while ensuring consumer safety.
