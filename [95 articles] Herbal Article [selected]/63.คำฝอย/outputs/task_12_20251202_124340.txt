Additionally, the mechanism of injury associated with Carthamus tinctorius is not well understood, and further research is needed to ensure the safe use of the plant and its extracts.

However, it is crucial to conduct thorough research and testing to ensure the safety and efficacy of these products. By carefully considering the technical feasibility, safety, and toxicity constraints, and considering the market opportunity and cultural context, it is possible to develop innovative and effective products based on Carthamus tinctorius that meet the needs of consumers and contribute to the growth of the herbal supplement industry.
