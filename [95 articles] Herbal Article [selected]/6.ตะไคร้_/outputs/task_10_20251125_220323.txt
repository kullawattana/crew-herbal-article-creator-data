# ===CULTURAL_INTERNAL_SUMMARY=== Lemongrass, also known as ตะไคร้, is a herb commonly used in Thai cuisine and traditional medicine. It has been used for healing purposes in Thai wisdom for a long time. Lemongrass is known for its citrusy flavor and aroma, and is often used in soups, curries, and other dishes. In traditional Thai medicine, lemongrass is believed to have various health benefits, including reducing inflammation, improving digestion, and relieving stress. It is also used as a natural remedy for fever, colds, and coughs. Additionally, lemongrass is used in traditional Thai massage to help relieve muscle tension and improve circulation. Overall, lemongrass is a versatile and fragrant herb that has been an integral part of Thai culture and traditional medicine for centuries.
