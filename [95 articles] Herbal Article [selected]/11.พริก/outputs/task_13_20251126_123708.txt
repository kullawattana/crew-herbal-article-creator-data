## Traditional Wisdom
Chili peppers have been used for centuries in traditional Thai medicine and cuisine. In Thai culture, chili peppers are known as "พริก" (phrik) and are believed to have medicinal properties, including stimulating digestion and relieving symptoms of various ailments. They are also used as a condiment in traditional Thai dishes, such as tom yum soup, green curry, and som tam (papaya salad). The cultural significance of chili peppers in Thai cuisine and traditional medicine is evident in the various communities that have developed unique recipes and remedies using chili peppers. For example, the Central Thai community uses chili peppers to add flavor and heat to various dishes, while the Southern Thai community is known for its unique chili pepper-based recipes.

## Safety, Regulatory, and Constraints
Capsicum annuum, also known as cayenne pepper, is generally recognized as safe (GRAS) as a food by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. However, it may cause skin irritation, pain, and bleeding in some individuals. The mechanism of injury is not well understood, but it is thought to be related to the capsaicin content of the plant.
