# ===CULTURAL_INTERNAL_SUMMARY=== Chili peppers, known as พริก in Thai, have been used for centuries in Thai wisdom for their medicinal properties and as a condiment in Thai cuisine. According to traditional Thai medicine, chili peppers have a hot and spicy flavor that helps to stimulate digestion and relieve symptoms of various ailments. They are also believed to have antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, which can help to boost the immune system and prevent diseases. In addition, chili peppers are a rich source of vitamins and minerals, including vitamin C, vitamin B6, and potassium. In Thai cuisine, chili peppers are commonly used as an ingredient in dishes such as tom yum soup, green curry, and som tam (papaya salad). They are also used as a condiment to add flavor and heat to various dishes. Overall, chili peppers are a versatile and nutritious ingredient that has been an integral part of Thai cuisine and traditional medicine for centuries.
