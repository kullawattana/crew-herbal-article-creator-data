    * **community_name:** Ban Khok, Ban Na Law, U Thong District, Suphan Buri Province
    * **location:** U Thong District, Suphan Buri Province
    * **ethnic_group:** Villagers
* **community_profile_summaries:**
    * **community_highlight:** The community of Ban Khok and Ban Na Law in U Thong District, Suphan Buri Province, has a rich tradition of using natural resources for various purposes. One of the plants used by the community is the Peppercorn Vine (เถาวัลย์เปรียง), which is a type of climber that grows in the area. The community uses the vine's bark to make rope.
    * **name_etymology:** The name "Peppercorn Vine" is derived from the plant's resemblance to peppercorns. The name "เถาวัลย์เปรียง" is a combination of two Thai words: "เถา" meaning "vine" and "วัลย์เปรียง" meaning "peppercorn-like".
    * **community_environment:** The community of Ban Khok and Ban Na Law is surrounded by a lush forest, which provides a rich source of natural resources. The community has developed a strong connection with the environment and uses the resources sustainably.
    * **local_wisdom:** The community has a deep understanding of the local flora and fauna, which is passed down through generations. The use of the Peppercorn Vine is an example of the community's traditional knowledge and skills.
    * **community_organizations:** The community has a strong social structure, with various organizations and groups that work together to manage the natural resources and promote sustainable development.
* **specific_entities:**
    * **local_names:**
        * **Item 1:**
            * **name:** Peppercorn Vine (เถาวัลย์เปรียง)
            * **context_quote:** The Peppercorn Vine is a type of climber that grows in the area and is used by the community to make rope.
    * **traditional_uses:**
        * **Item 1:**
            * **use:** Making rope from the vine's bark
            * **context_quote:** The community uses the Peppercorn Vine's bark to make rope, which is a traditional practice passed down through generations.
    * **processing_methods:**
        * **Item 1:**
            * **method:** Using the vine's bark to make rope
            * **description_quote:** The community uses the Peppercorn Vine's bark to make rope by cutting and drying the bark, then twisting it into rope.
