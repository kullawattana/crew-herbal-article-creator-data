## Safety, Regulatory, and Constraints
Cinnamomum verum is generally recognized as safe (GRAS) for use as a flavoring by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. However, it is crucial to distinguish Cinnamomum verum (Ceylon cinnamon) from other forms of cinnamon, such as cassia or Chinese cinnamon, which contain high amounts of the toxic ingredient coumarin. Common side effects of consuming Cinnamomum verum include bloating, dyspepsia, heartburn, and nausea. The lack of data on the safety of cinnamon in nursing mothers or infants is a significant constraint that must be addressed through further research or clear labeling to avoid these consumer groups.
