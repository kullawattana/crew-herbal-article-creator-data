### Overall Summary
* **summary:** Etlingera elatior has been traditionally used to alleviate stomach discomfort, and its inflorescence extract has been reported to possess anti-inflammatory activity. The extract has been shown to downregulate the expression of inducible nitric oxide synthase (iNOS) in ethanol-induced gastric ulcer rats. Quercetin, a flavonoid compound found in Etlingera elatior, has been identified as a potential anti-inflammatory agent.
