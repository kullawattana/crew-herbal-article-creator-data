# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Pandanus tectorius * **herb_name:** Pandanus tectorius * **source_browsed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32154156/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** Pandanus tectorius fruit has been found to have antihypercholesterolemic and antiatherosclerotic potencies via the regulation of high-density lipoprotein (HDL) receptor (scavenger receptor-B1) gene expression and 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl coenzyme A reductase reductase activity. * **mechanism_of_injury:** The mechanism of injury is not fully understood, but it is believed that the fruit's active compounds may help to increase HDL cholesterol levels and reduce total cholesterol levels. * **reported_side_effects:** No significant side effects have been reported. * **case_report_summary:** There are no reported case studies on the toxicity of Pandanus tectorius fruit.
