## Scientific Deep-Dive

Studies have shown that the ethanol extract (EP) of Eclipta and its fractions have lipid-lowering activity and antioxidant properties. The LD50 of EPF3, a fraction of the ethanol extract, was undetectable, suggesting a relatively safe profile.

## Traditional Wisdom
In traditional Thai medicine, Eclipta prostrata is known as "Kameng" and is used to prepare medicine. The herb is ground into a powder and consumed with Kanom Jeen (fermented rice noodles). The community of Chunchon Takhiantong in Chanthaburi Province has a rich cultural heritage and uses Eclipta prostrata in traditional medicine. The herb is believed to have antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties, making it effective in treating various ailments such as fever, rheumatism, and skin conditions.

## Safety, Regulatory, and Constraints
The toxicity facts indicate that Eclipta prostrata has been reported to have hypolipidemic effects, and its mechanism of action is not well understood.
