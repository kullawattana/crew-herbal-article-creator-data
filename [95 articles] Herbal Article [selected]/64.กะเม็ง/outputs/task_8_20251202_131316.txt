    * **name_etymology:** The name "Takhiantong" is derived from the abundance of Takhian trees in the area. The community was previously known as "Ban Tayod" but was later renamed to "Ban Takhiantong".

    * **local_wisdom:** The community has a rich tradition of herbal medicine, with a wide range of plants used for various ailments. The villagers have developed unique processing methods and recipes to prepare the herbs for consumption.
    * **community_organizations:** The community has a strong social structure, with various organizations and groups working together to promote cultural heritage and traditional practices.
