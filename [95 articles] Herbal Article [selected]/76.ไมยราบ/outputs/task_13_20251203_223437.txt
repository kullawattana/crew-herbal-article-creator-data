## Scientific Deep-Dive
Mimosa pudica, also known as Sensitive Plant, has been found to have hypolipidemic activity, with significant reduction in the level of serum cholesterol, triglyceride, LDL, VLDL, and increase in HDL level. The chloroform extract of Mimosa pudica leaves has exhibited significant atherogenic index and percentage protection against hyperlipidemia.
