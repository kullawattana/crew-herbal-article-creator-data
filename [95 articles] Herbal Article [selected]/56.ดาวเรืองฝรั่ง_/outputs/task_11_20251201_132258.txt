When the older brother fell asleep, the younger brother saw a firefly crawling out of his brother's nose and disappearing into the termite mound. Later, the younger brother fell asleep and had a dream in which a spirit told him where to find a treasure. The spirit asked him to dig up the treasure and sell it to build a temple. When the brothers woke up, they dug up the termite mound and found a lot of treasure. They sold the treasure and used the money to build a temple, which they named "Wat Jet Phut" (วัดเจตภูติ). Later, the name was changed to "Wat Ta Phut" (วัดตะพูด) and finally to "Wat Tha Phut" (วัดท่าพูด).
