## Safety, Regulatory, and Constraints
The safety and regulatory aspects of Catharanthus roseus are critical due to the known toxicity of its vinca alkaloids. These compounds can cause peripheral neuropathy, gastrointestinal distress, hematologic toxicity, cardiovascular effects, seizures, and syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion. Additionally, case reports of liver injury, hepatotoxicity, and hepatic veno-occlusive disease associated with vinca alkaloids necessitate careful consideration of dosage limits and safety warnings. Any product development must prioritize addressing these safety concerns through rigorous testing, appropriate dosing, and clear warning labels to mitigate risks to consumers.
