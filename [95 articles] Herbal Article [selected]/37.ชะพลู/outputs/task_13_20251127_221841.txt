## Scientific Deep-Dive into Piper Sarmentosum
### Scientific Research
Piper sarmentosum has been the subject of several scientific studies, highlighting its potential health benefits. The ethyl acetate fraction of Piper sarmentosum extracts has shown strong xanthine oxidase inhibition and antioxidant activity. Additionally, compounds like chrysin have been identified with notable XO inhibition, outperforming some conventional drugs. These findings suggest that Piper sarmentosum extracts could be developed into products with significant health benefits.

> **Scientific Research:** 
> The ethyl acetate fraction (PLEA) exhibited the strongest XO inhibition (IC50 value of 13.90 ± 0.82 μg/mL) and antioxidant activity. PLE50 and PLE96 significantly reduced carrageenan-induced paw edema in mice. Among the seven isolated compounds, chrysin displayed outstanding XO inhibition (IC50 value of 2.05 ± 0.04 μM), outperforming allopurinol (IC50 = 4.80 ± 0.11 μM), along with notable NO and TNF-α inhibition. LC/MS-GNPS revealed differential metabolite profiles in PLE50 and PLE96 extracts, highlighting the influence of solvent polarity on pharmacological activity.
> 
> Another study found that increasing antibiotic resistance in Mycoplasma hyopneumoniae (Mhp) necessitates alternative treatments. Piper sarmentosum extracts (PSE) and guava extracts (GE) show promise due to their antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. The combined in vitro effects of PSE and GE against Mhp and their antioxidant activities were assessed, showing that PSE contained vitexin-2-O-rhamnoside and pellitorine, while GE had quercetin-3-O-glucuronide, quercetin, isoquercitrin, and avicularin. The 1:1 combination of PSE and GE demonstrated superior anti-Mhp activity and enhanced antioxidant properties compared to the individual extracts.
> 
> A third study aimed to investigate the phytochemical composition and biological activities of P. sarmentosum extracts. The results showed that the extracts contained various phytochemicals, including flavonoids, phenolic acids, and alkaloids, and exhibited antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial activities, suggesting their potential as natural remedies for various diseases.

## Traditional Wisdom of Piper Sarmentosum
Piper sarmentosum has been used in traditional Thai medicine for its health benefits, including aiding in digestion and relieving symptoms of certain illnesses.
