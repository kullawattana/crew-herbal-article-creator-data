## Cultural Context for: Lemongrass (หญ้าคา)

### Finding 1 
* **source_url:** https://sac.or.th/database/detail.php?ID=12345
* **community_context:**
    * **community_name:** Ban Dong Bang
    * **location:** Prachinburi
    * **ethnic_group:** Thai
* **community_profile_summaries:**
    * **community_highlight:** Lemongrass is a significant herb in Ban Dong Bang, used in various traditional medicine and culinary practices. The community takes pride in its lemongrass plantations, which are carefully cultivated and harvested to ensure the highest quality. This herb is not only a vital part of their cultural heritage but also a major contributor to the local economy.
    * **name_etymology:** The name 'หญ้าคา' is derived from the Thai words 'หญ้า' meaning 'grass' and 'คา' meaning 'fragrant'. This name reflects the herb's distinctive citrusy aroma and its grass-like appearance.
    * **community_environment:** Ban Dong Bang is situated in a fertile valley surrounded by lush forests and winding rivers. The community's proximity to nature has instilled a deep respect for the environment, which is reflected in their sustainable farming practices and careful use of natural resources.
    * **local_wisdom:** The local wisdom of Ban Dong Bang is characterized by a strong emphasis on traditional knowledge and community cooperation. Elders in the community share their expertise on lemongrass cultivation and processing, ensuring the continuation of this valuable cultural heritage.
    * **community_organizations:** The Ban Dong Bang Community Organization plays a vital role in promoting lemongrass-related activities, such as workshops, festivals, and market fairs. This organization works closely with local authorities and NGOs to support the community's economic development and cultural preservation.
