## Traditional Wisdom
Imperata cylindrica has significant cultural and traditional importance in communities like Ban Dong Bang and Ban Khao Yai. In Ban Dong Bang, it is used to relieve stomach pain, while in Ban Khao Yai, it is used as an insect repellent. The herb is also known by different local names, such as Khamin Chan in Ban Dong Bang and Serai in Ban Khao Yai.
