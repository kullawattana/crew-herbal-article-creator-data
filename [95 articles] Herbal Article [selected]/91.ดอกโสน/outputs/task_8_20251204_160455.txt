### Finding 1
* **source_url:** https://southeastasia.sac.or.th/subject.php?c_id=2&sj_id=37
* **community_context:**
    * **community_name:** Klong Pho Community
    * **location:** Sam Ruean Subdistrict, Bang Pa-in District, Ayutthaya Province
    * **ethnic_group:** Local Thai
* **community_profile_summaries:**
    * **community_highlight:** The Klong Pho community is known for its traditional way of life, with a strong emphasis on agriculture and water-based transportation. The community is surrounded by natural canals and waterways, making it an ideal location for farming and fishing.
    * **name_etymology:** The name "Klong Pho" is derived from the Thai word for "canal," which reflects the community's reliance on water-based transportation and agriculture.
    * **community_environment:** The community is situated in a low-lying area with a high water table, making it prone to flooding during the rainy season. However, this also makes it an ideal location for farming and fishing.
    * **local_wisdom:** The community has a rich tradition of folk wisdom, with many residents possessing knowledge of traditional medicine, agriculture, and craftsmanship.
    * **community_organizations:** The community has several organizations that promote traditional culture and way of life, including the Klong Pho Community Development Center.

    * **local_names:**
        * **Item 1:**
            * **name:** Dok Khao (ดอกโสน)
            * **context_quote:** Dok Khao is a type of flower that is commonly found in the Klong Pho community.
    * **traditional_uses:**
        * **Item 1:**
            * **use:** Dok Khao is used as an ingredient in traditional desserts.
            * **context_quote:** Dok Khao is an essential ingredient in traditional desserts such as Khanom Dok Khao.
    * **processing_methods:**
        * **Item 1:**
            * **method:** Dok Khao is used in traditional cooking methods such as steaming and boiling.
