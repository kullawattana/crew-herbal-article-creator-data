# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Neptunia oleracea * **herb_name:** Neptunia oleracea * **source_browsed:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7694506/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** Neptunia oleracea has been identified as a feasible phytoremediator for cleaning up aquatic systems contaminated with arsenic. The plant's tolerance to arsenic was observed up to 30 ppm, and the highest arsenic accumulation and removal efficacy were observed at the range of 30–60 ppm. However, increasing the arsenic concentrations above 60 ppm caused severe damage and death of the plants. * **mechanism_of_injury:** The mechanism of injury is not explicitly stated in the article, but it is mentioned that arsenic can cause oxidative stress, decrease photosynthesis rate, and inhibit growth parameters in plants. * **reported_side_effects:** The reported side effects of arsenic toxicity in Neptunia oleracea include deformation of tissues, necrosis, chlorosis, and yellowing of leaves, as well as root hardening and woody formation. * **case_report_summary:** There are no human case reports mentioned in the article. The study focused on the phytoremediation potential of Neptunia oleracea for removing arsenic from aquatic systems.
