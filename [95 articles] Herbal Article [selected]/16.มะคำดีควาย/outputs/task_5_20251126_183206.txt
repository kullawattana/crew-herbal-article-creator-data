* **uses:** helps prevent sunburn, if used as directed with other sun protection measures (see Directions), decreases the risk of skin cancer and skin aging caused by the sun
* **warnings:** For external use only, Do not use on damaged broken skin, When using this product keep out of eyes. Rinse with water to remove, Stop use and ask a doctor if rash occurs., Keep out of reach of children., If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
* **directions:** apply liberally 15 minutes before sun exposure, children less than 6 months of age: ask a doctor, reapply at least every 2 hours, use a water resistant sunscreen if swimming or sweating
* **other_information:** protect the product in this container from excessive heat and direct sun, the product may stain certain types of fabrics
