## Scientific Deep-Dive into Calotropis gigantea
Calotropis gigantea, a plant with potential medicinal properties, has been studied for its compounds, including Uscharin, Calotoxin, Calotropin, Calactin, and Calotropage. The dichloromethane extract from its leaves has shown strong cytotoxic activity against non-small cell lung carcinoma (A549), colon carcinoma (HCT 116), and hepatocellular carcinoma (Hep G2), while being non-toxic to Chinese hamster ovary (AA8) cells. This suggests potential applications in pharmaceutical or nutraceutical products.
