# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Parkia speciosa * **herb_name:** Parkia speciosa * **source_browsed:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548231/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** Parkia speciosa has been found to have antioxidant and cardioprotective activities, as well as antibacterial properties against human pathogenic bacteria. However, its toxicity study is also lacking, and no in vivo toxicity study has been carried out. Only Aisha et al. had performed a cytotoxicity study of the plant using HUVEC. In their study, the methanolic extract of the fresh pods (100 μg/mL) did not show any significant cytotoxic effect on the cell line. * **mechanism_of_injury:** The cause of liver injury due to Parkia speciosa is unknown. It is often used with other agents, including drugs of abuse, and its causative relationship to liver injury in published cases is not always clear. * **reported_side_effects:** Nausea, dermatitis, rash, diarrhea, dysentery, hypertension, cough, chronic piles, conjunctivitis, and measles. * **case_report_summary:** Case reports of liver injury in patients taking Parkia speciosa have been reported, with symptoms including fever, fatigue, and jaundice. The liver injury is typically cholestatic or mixed, and can be severe with serum bilirubin levels rising above 20 mg/dL. The severe cholestasis can be accompanied by acute renal failure and bone marrow toxicity.
