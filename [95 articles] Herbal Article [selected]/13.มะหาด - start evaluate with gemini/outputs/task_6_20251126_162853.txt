# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Artocarpus lacucha * **herb_name:** Artocarpus lacucha * **source_browsed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37289434/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** Artocarpus lacucha has been found to have insecticidal activity against Spodoptera litura larvae. The ethyl acetate crude extract of A. lacucha was the most toxic to second-instar S. litura larvae, with a 24-h LD50 value of ~9.07 µg/larva. The catechin isolated from the ethyl acetate crude extract exhibited the highest toxicity against this insect, with a 24-h LD50 value of ~8.37 µg/larva. * **mechanism_of_injury:** The mechanism of injury is not explicitly stated in the article. However, it is mentioned that the catechin isolated from A. lacucha decreased the activities of acetylcholinesterase, carboxylesterases, and glutathione S-transferase in the larvae. * **reported_side_effects:** No side effects are mentioned in the article. * **case_report_summary:** No case reports are mentioned in the article.
