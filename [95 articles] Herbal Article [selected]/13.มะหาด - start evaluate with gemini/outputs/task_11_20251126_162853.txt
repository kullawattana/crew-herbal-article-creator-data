* **Science Fact (Abstracts):**
  * **Study 1:**
    * **abstract_raw:** Artocarpus lacucha has been found to have insecticidal activity against Spodoptera litura larvae. The ethyl acetate crude extract of A. lacucha was the most toxic to second-instar S. litura larvae, with a 24-h LD50 value of ~9.07 µg/larva. The catechin isolated from the ethyl acetate crude extract exhibited the highest toxicity against this insect, with a 24-h LD50 value of ~8.37 µg/larva.
