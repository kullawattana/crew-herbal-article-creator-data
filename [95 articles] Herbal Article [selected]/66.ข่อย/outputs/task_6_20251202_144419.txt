# ===TOXICITY_DATA=== ## Clinical Toxicity Data for: Streblus asper * **herb_name:** Streblus asper * **source_browsed:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5745612/ ### Clinical Summary * **toxicity_summary:** Ethanolic extract of Streblus asper leaves protects against glutamate-induced toxicity in HT22 hippocampal neuronal cells and extends lifespan of Caenorhabditis elegans. The extract possesses both longevity effects and neuroprotective activity against glutamate-induced cell death, supporting its therapeutic potential for the treatment of age-associated neurodegenerative diseases. * **mechanism_of_injury:** The underlying mechanism of neuroprotection involves the activation of the Nrf2 signaling pathway, which promotes antioxidant defense by up-regulating the expression of antioxidant-related genes such as NQO1, GCLM, and EAAT3. * **reported_side_effects:** None reported in the study. * **case_report_summary:** No human case reports were mentioned in the study.
