## Traditional Wisdom
In traditional Thai medicine, Moringa oleifera (มะรุม) has been used for healing purposes for a long time. It is believed to have various health benefits, including aiding digestion and relieving symptoms of certain diseases. The community of Ban Dong Bang in Prachinburi Province is known for its rich cultural heritage and traditional wisdom related to herbs, including Moringa oleifera. The name "Ban Dong Bang" itself reflects the community's connection to herbs, with "ban" meaning village, "dong" meaning herbs, and "bang" meaning place. The community's strengths lie in preserving traditional knowledge and passing it down to younger generations. They have a deep understanding of the local ecosystem and have developed sustainable practices to preserve the environment. The use of Moringa oleifera in traditional Thai medicine for relieving stomach pain, processed through drying, highlights its practical application in local wisdom.

## Safety, Regulatory, and Constraints
Moringa oleifera leaf extract is used as an active ingredient with a skin protectant purpose. However, it is crucial to follow the safety guidelines: it is for external use only, should not be used on eczema or scalp wounds, and users should be cautious of signs of hypersensitivity. Additionally, it should not be used internally, and contact with eyes should be avoided. Storage in direct sunlight or near fire is also not recommended. These constraints are vital for the safe use of Moringa oleifera products, ensuring that users can benefit from its potential while minimizing risks.
