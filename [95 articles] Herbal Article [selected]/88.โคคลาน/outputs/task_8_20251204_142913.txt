* **community_context:**
    * **community_name:** Ban Dong Bang 
    * **location:** Prachinburi Province 
    * **ethnic_group:** Thai 
* **community_profile_summaries:**
    * **community_highlight:** Ban Dong Bang is renowned for its expertise in traditional herbal medicine, particularly in the treatment of digestive issues. The community has developed unique remedies using locally sourced herbs, which have been passed down through generations. The villagers take great pride in their knowledge, which has been recognized by the Princess Maha Chakri Sirindhorn Anthropology Centre. 
    * **name_etymology:** The name "Ban Dong Bang" is derived from the Thai words "dong" meaning "swamp" and "bang" meaning "village." According to local legend, the village was founded by a group of settlers who discovered a swampy area rich in medicinal herbs. Over time, the community developed a deep understanding of the local flora and its uses. 
    * **community_environment:** Ban Dong Bang is situated in a lush, tropical region with an abundance of plant species. The community has developed a strong connection with the natural environment, which is reflected in their traditional practices. The villagers believe in living in harmony with nature and have developed sustainable methods for harvesting and cultivating herbs. 
    * **local_wisdom:** The community has a rich tradition of passing down knowledge and skills from one generation to the next. Elders in the community play a vital role in sharing their expertise and experiences with younger members, ensuring the continuation of traditional practices. 
    * **community_organizations:** Ban Dong Bang has established a community-based organization to promote and preserve traditional herbal knowledge. The organization works closely with local authorities and experts to develop sustainable projects and initiatives that benefit the community.
