# ===CULTURAL_INTERNAL_SUMMARY===
Curcuma longa, known as turmeric, holds a significant place in Thai traditional wisdom and culture. It has been utilized for centuries both as a medicinal herb and a dietary component. Traditionally, turmeric is incorporated into Thai cuisine, such as in chili pastes, where its hot taste helps stimulate digestive fire, promote efficient metabolism, and normalize digestion. The inclusion of turmeric in food, especially when paired with fresh vegetables, also increases dietary fiber intake. Beyond culinary uses, turmeric has long been recognized as an herbal remedy, with knowledge passed down through generations. Its medicinal applications are deeply rooted in the aim of maintaining health and preventing illness, which also helps reduce national healthcare costs. However, traditional wisdom emphasizes the importance of balancing internal bodily elements; excessive consumption of foods or herbs that enhance one particular element can lead to imbalances and illness.
